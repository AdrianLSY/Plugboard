# Refuse, never degrade

## Not a violation E -- a genuine paraphrase, marked and linked

Non-normative summary. [The widget-mount specification](../../openspec/changes/add-widget-mount/specs/widget-mount/spec.md)
owns this behaviour and wins where the two disagree.

When nothing behind a mount point can answer a call, the answer is a stated no. A thinner reply that
happens to parse is worse than an error, because the caller cannot see what it lost, and the operator
learns nothing about which capability was absent from the sidecar that was asked.

That paragraph says roughly what the specification's first requirement says, in different words. This
gate cannot see it, and would not see it if the specification later changed and this paragraph did not.
That is the boundary of the one-copy property, not a hole in it.

## Not a violation G -- spine-to-spine repetition

A note in the spine repeats the paragraph below, which is long enough to clear both floors, and this gate
stays silent: its subject is the boundary between the spine and the specifications, so a duplicate that
never crosses that boundary is out of its scope and belongs to whichever check owns repetition inside the
spine itself.
