# Sidecar connection trace

This traces the **planned contract** from sidecar startup to departure. The graph built for this project excludes `openspec/`, so the graph supplied the conceptual path and the owning specifications supply the behavior. The current `proxy` and `sidecar` tunnel packages are skeletons; there is no implemented connection path to run yet. See [`proxy/lib/plugboard.ex`](../proxy/lib/plugboard.ex), [`sidecar/internal/tunnel/tunnel.go`](../sidecar/internal/tunnel/tunnel.go), and [`contract/README.md`](../contract/README.md).

Graph vocabulary expansion used for the initial traversal: `listener`, `connection`, `registration`, `registry`, `capability`, `declaration`, `refusal`, `disconnect`, `lifecycle`, `credential`, `handshake`, `sidecar`.

## Establishment

```text
sidecar configuration validation
  -> local diagnostic surface
  -> verify listener identity and protection
  -> acquire and present credential
  -> declare framing, version range, parameters and capabilities
  -> authenticate and negotiate one mount
  -> create one registry entry for one tunnel
  -> assess backend reachability and report readiness
```

1. The sidecar starts in that order. Its local liveness and readiness probes answer before the tunnel exists. A backend that is down prevents readiness but does not prevent tunnel establishment. [Sidecar startup](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-startup-is-an-ordered-sequence-with-observable-intermediate-states), [backend reachability](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-backend-reachability-is-proven-by-the-sidecar-and-never-inferred).
2. The listener presents its own verifiable identity and establishes confidentiality and integrity **before** reading a declaration or credential. The sidecar does not even materialize its credential until endpoint verification succeeds, and it does not follow a redirection to another host. Framing is identified explicitly at connection setup; the contract version is negotiated separately in band. [Listener identity](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-the-listener-presents-a-verifiable-endpoint-identity-on-every-connection), [protected connection](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-the-connection-is-confidential-and-integrity-protected-before-anything-is-read), [framing](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-the-framing-spoken-over-the-connection-is-identified-explicitly-at-setup), [credential materialization](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-the-credential-is-not-materialised-until-the-endpoint-has-verified).
3. Pre-authentication admission has per-instance and per-observed-source connection limits, one total time budget across all pre-authentication phases, and an octet bound. Connection phases distinguish accepted, protection, framing, awaiting declaration, credential evaluation, established, draining, and ended. The peer sees an opaque authentication failure; the operator sees a specific cause. [Pre-authentication bounds](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-connections-in-the-pre-authentication-state-are-bounded), [phase vocabulary](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-every-connection-is-attributable-to-an-enumerated-phase-while-it-is-open), [authentication failure](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-authentication-failures-are-indistinguishable-to-the-presenter).
4. The first frame from each peer is its bounded establishment declaration. The peers choose the highest overlapping contract version, fix connection parameters and capability sets for that tunnel, and refuse malformed or incompatible declarations. A credential authenticates the tunnel once, binding its credential identifier, tenant, and one mount for its lifetime; client requests cannot widen that scope. [Version negotiation](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-contract-version-negotiation), [ordered exchange](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-tunnel-establishment-is-a-bounded-ordered-exchange), [capabilities](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-capability-declaration), [tunnel authentication](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-authentication-binds-to-the-tunnel-not-to-individual-requests).
5. The registry admits the tunnel only for its credential's **live mount point in the same tenant**. One tunnel yields one entry; an entry records tenant, mount, negotiated version, capabilities, unique proxy-assigned ID, and holding instance. A multi-mount deployment needs one tunnel per mount. Eligibility starts only after establishment, authentication, and negotiation. [Registration](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-registration-is-authorised-against-a-live-mount-of-the-credentials-tenant), [entry](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-registry-entry-describes-one-tunnel), [eligibility](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-eligibility-begins-and-ends-with-the-tunnel).

## Serving an exchange

```text
client request -> one tenant-scoped mount match -> required capabilities
  -> reachable, non-draining, capable registry entry -> holding instance
  -> one stream on that entry's tunnel -> sidecar's configured backend origin
  -> response frames and completion, or an enumerated reset/failure
```

1. Routing matches at most one mount by segment-boundary prefix and computes a remainder without rewriting its octets. No match ends here: no registry selection or tunnel exchange begins. [Mount match](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/routing/mount-points/spec.md#requirement-a-request-matches-at-most-one-mount-by-longest-prefix), [remainder](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/routing/mount-points/spec.md#requirement-the-forwarded-remainder-is-the-request-target-minus-the-mount-prefix).
2. The required capability set is determined from the request head and route class **before** dispatch. The registry filters all entries by that set before selection. A missing capability is named in a refusal; zero eligible entries and entries that exist but cannot be reached from the selecting instance have distinct reasons. An affinity key is proxy-derived, tenant-and-mount scoped, bounded, and cannot name an entry or override capability filtering. [Refusal](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-refusal-rather-than-silent-degradation), [filtering](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-eligibility-is-filtered-by-capability-before-selection-not-after), [selection](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-selection-is-total-deterministic-in-outcome-and-never-unreachable), [affinity](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-affinity-bindings-are-registry-state-with-a-bounded-lifetime).
3. Any instance may select an entry whose tunnel another instance holds. The exchange then crosses at most one internal dispatch hop; neither client nor sidecar is told about it, and cancellation, credit, ordering, fields, and failure reasons survive the hop. [Cross-instance service](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-sidecar-connected-to-one-instance-serves-requests-arriving-at-any-instance), [dispatch hop](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-cross-instance-dispatch-crosses-a-surface-of-its-own), [equivalence](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-dispatched-exchange-is-indistinguishable-from-one-admitted-at-the-holding-instance).
4. Each exchange names the mount it matched; the sidecar resets a request head naming a different mount without issuing a backend request. The exchange uses frames on a stream identifier scoped to this tunnel. Heads, body octets, interim responses, trailers, resets, and explicit body completion can be interleaved with other streams. Where streaming and backpressure were declared, transfer starts before the whole body exists and credit propagates the downstream consumer's pace. [Mount check](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-each-exchange-names-the-mount-it-was-matched-to), [frames](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-framed-exchange-with-stream-identifiers), [streaming](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-incremental-transfer-without-aggregation), [flow control](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-credit-based-flow-control).
5. The sidecar dials only the locally configured scheme, host, and port. It validates the carried remainder before opening a backend connection, refuses unusable targets rather than repairing them, and preserves the query. Backend failures have distinct contract error codes; a response already begun is truncated rather than replaced by a synthetic success or error body. [Origin](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-the-backend-origin-is-taken-only-from-local-configuration), [target](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-the-remainder-is-validated-before-a-connection-is-opened-and-rejection-is-never-repair), [backend failures](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-backend-failures-are-distinct-outcomes-the-contract-can-carry).

### Exchange-level forks

| Fork | Outcome |
|---|---|
| No mount match; no eligible sidecar; eligible but unreachable; no capable entry | Four distinct conditions. No match starts no exchange; the others produce enumerated reasons without waiting for a timeout. [Mount match](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/routing/mount-points/spec.md#requirement-a-request-matches-at-most-one-mount-by-longest-prefix), [selection](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-selection-is-total-deterministic-in-outcome-and-never-unreachable), [capability filtering](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-eligibility-is-filtered-by-capability-before-selection-not-after), [no sidecar](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-no-sidecar-available-is-its-own-outcome). |
| Affinity-bound entry unreachable from an instance | Installation policy is exactly **refuse** or **rebind**. Refuse names the unreachable binding; rebind selects a reachable capable entry and records the move. A transient partition does not masquerade as binding expiry. [Unreachable binding](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-an-affinity-binding-whose-entry-is-unreachable-has-a-declared-outcome). |
| Internal dispatch hop fails after selection | It has its own enumerated reason, attributable to the admitting and holding instances. A response already begun is incomplete, and the sidecar learns no instance topology. [Hop failure](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-failure-of-the-dispatch-hop-is-its-own-reason). |
| Client cancels or backend fails after a response starts | A stream reset propagates cancellation to the backend; a backend failure after commitment truncates the client response. Other streams remain unaffected. [Cancellation](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-cancellation-propagates-in-both-directions), [backend failure](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-backend-failures-are-distinct-outcomes-the-contract-can-carry). |
| Bad exchange frame versus broken framing | A well-formed but unusable stream frame resets that stream; untrustworthy frame boundaries fail the whole tunnel. [Framing classification](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-malformed-framing-is-tunnel-fatal-and-unusable-frames-are-stream-fatal). |

Each exchange releases its state when both independently closing directions have ended, or either side resets it. A final response head alone is not completion. [Half-close](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-each-direction-of-an-exchange-closes-independently).

## Every connection-level ending found

| Cause | Required end state |
|---|---|
| Identity, protection, framing, pre-authentication bound, credential, version, declaration, or mount admission fails | Refusal before eligibility and before any request is served. The refusal class is recorded; authentication causes remain indistinguishable to the presenter. [Listener](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-a-refusal-is-specific-to-the-operator-and-opaque-to-an-unauthenticated-peer), [registry](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-eligibility-begins-and-ends-with-the-tunnel). |
| Sidecar contract logic freezes or network disappears | Contract-level liveness fails even if the transport looks open; entry becomes ineligible, in-flight exchanges fail promptly with sidecar-unavailability, and reconnect uses independently jittered backoff. An underlying backend failure alone does **not** remove an otherwise live entry. [Liveness](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-liveness-is-proven-end-to-end-not-inferred-from-the-transport), [fan-out](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-departure-fails-in-flight-exchanges-promptly-and-distinguishably), [reconnect](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-re-establishment-after-a-lost-tunnel-is-backed-off-and-spread). |
| Selected entry fails **before delivery** | A different eligible entry may be tried, excluding prior attempts and within a configured attempt bound. After any part of an exchange was delivered, it is never replayed to another entry; it fails instead. [Reselection](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-reselection-is-bounded-and-never-revisits-an-attempted-entry). |
| Sidecar drains or shuts down | New work stops for that entry, existing exchanges get time to finish, the deadline bounds the wait, and the tunnel closes with planned-withdrawal reason. Its same-tunnel drain cannot be reversed. [Drain](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-sidecar-can-drain-without-failing-its-in-flight-work), [shutdown](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-shutdown-withdraws-readiness-first-releases-the-backend-hop-and-exits-successfully). |
| Proxy instance withdraws | Readiness withdraws, new tunnels and exchanges stop, work is given a completion period, remaining endings are spread, and sidecars reconnect at surviving instances. [Withdrawal](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-planned-proxy-withdrawal-does-not-disconnect-a-fleet-at-once). |
| Credential expires, is revoked, or its mount is deleted | New authentication fails and established tunnels terminate within a bound; in-flight exchanges fail explicitly. A revoked credential is not retried. During credential-store outage, new authentication fails closed but established tunnels continue; revocation/expiry is enforced on recovery. [Expiry](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-expiry-is-enforced-at-presentation), [revocation](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-revocation-takes-effect-for-future-and-established-connections), [store outage](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-an-unreachable-credential-store-fails-authentication-closed-without-severing-service), [mount lifecycle](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-mount-lifecycle-bounds-credential-validity). |
| Credential rotates | Existing tunnel and exchanges continue under their original authenticated identity; both credentials work during the overlap and the replacement is used on a future establishment. [Rotation](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-rotation-replaces-a-credential-without-interrupting-service), [fixed identity](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-tunnel-establishment-is-a-bounded-ordered-exchange). |

An operator may also suspend only the listener's acceptance of **new** connections while established tunnels keep serving; this is reversible, unlike instance withdrawal. A proxy partition temporarily prevents selection across it, then the registry converges to live tunnels when it heals. A reconnect creates an independent entry, and late removal of the old entry cannot remove the new one. [Listener suspension](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-the-listener-drains-by-ceasing-to-accept-while-established-tunnels-continue), [partition healing](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-the-registry-converges-after-a-partition-without-intervention), [superseded incarnation](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-a-superseded-incarnation-never-removes-its-successor).

## Adjacent branches and limits

- **Readiness and eligibility differ.** The sidecar reports unready if its backend is unreachable, but the registry's tunnel-level liveness does not probe the backend or remove the entry on that basis. Such a request can still select the sidecar and receive a backend failure. [Sidecar readiness](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/sidecar/program/spec.md#requirement-backend-reachability-is-proven-by-the-sidecar-and-never-inferred), [registry liveness](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-liveness-is-proven-end-to-end-not-inferred-from-the-transport).
- **Custom domains are an indirect graph connection, not a tunnel establishment step.** They govern client-facing hostname claims and certificates. Key custody also supplies the separate platform endpoint identity that the tunnel listener presents. [Custom domains](../docs/capabilities/routing-custom-domains.md), [key custody](../docs/capabilities/security-key-custody.md), [listener identity](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/listener/spec.md#requirement-the-listener-presents-a-verifiable-endpoint-identity-on-every-connection).
- **Conformance is a release claim, not a handshake gate.** A version/capability claim needs a conformance result before release; no result is requested or evaluated on the wire. [Version claim](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-a-version-claim-is-backed-by-conformance).
- **Protocol fidelity branches start after selection.** Ordered repeated headers, opaque body octets, interim responses, trailers, and bidirectional streams are carried by the same framed exchange. Datagram framing is reserved in v1 but its capability is undeclared; an exchange needing an undeclared optional capability is refused. These branches do not create a second registration path. [Header fields](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-header-fields-are-an-ordered-sequence-of-pairs), [bodies](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-bodies-are-opaque-octet-streams), [interim responses](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-interim-responses-precede-exactly-one-final-response), [trailers](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-trailer-sections-are-distinct-from-header-sections), [bidirectional streams](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-bidirectional-stream-exchanges-carry-the-backend-handshake), [datagrams](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/wire-contract/spec.md#requirement-datagram-class-is-defined-and-reserved).

## Unresolved specification collision

Routing requires an exchange already dispatched against a mount to continue after that mount is deleted, potentially remaining *retired-with-work-outstanding*. [Routing clause](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/routing/mount-points/spec.md#requirement-a-routing-change-never-retroactively-alters-an-exchange-already-dispatched). The registry says deleting that mount ends its tunnel, and the credential specification requires prompt termination of tunnels scoped to it. [Registry clause](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/tunnel/sidecar-registry/spec.md#requirement-registration-is-authorised-against-a-live-mount-of-the-credentials-tenant), [credential clause](../openspec/changes/rebuild-plugboard/specs/auth/sidecar-credentials/spec.md#requirement-mount-lifecycle-bounds-credential-validity). Those cannot all hold for a long-lived in-flight exchange on the deleted mount without a stated ordering or exception. Both sides are normative specifications at the same authority level; this trace leaves the outcome unresolved.
